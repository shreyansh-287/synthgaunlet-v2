              🛡️ What does SynthGauntlet do?
            
      
            Core Mission
          
      
          
      
            SynthGauntlet transforms sensitive, regulated datasets (customer analytics, healthcare records, financial metrics) into privacy-preserved synthetic datasets. The generated data looks, computes, and trains machine learning models exactly like real data—while guaranteeing that no real person can be re-identified or leaked.
          
      
          
      
            
      
              📊 High Analytical Utility
              
      Preserves multivariate correlations, statistical distributions, and high ML model accuracy (TSTR ≥ 85%).
      
            
      
            
      
              🕵️ Attacked Before Release
              
      Actively stress-tested by an AI adversary using linkage, nearest-neighbor, and membership inference attacks.
      
            
      
            
      
              📜 Cryptographic Audit Seal
              
      Every approved release receives an HMAC-signed tamper-evident PDF certificate for compliance verification.
      
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              📥 Step 1: Choose or Upload Your Dataset
            
      
            Data Ingestion
          
      
          
      
            You can load data into SynthGauntlet using any of three convenient options:
          
      
          
      
            
      
              
      
                1. File Upload
                CSV, XLSX, JSON, Parquet
                
      Drag and drop any tabular file up to 50,000 rows / 15 MB. An automated PII Pre-Scan detects direct identifiers (emails, phone numbers, SSNs, credit cards) and lets you Mask, Drop, or Salted Hash them before synthesis begins.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      
                2. Benchmark Samples
                Instant 1-Click Load
                
      Instantly test with pre-loaded real-world datasets: Adult Income (demographic census & income), Diabetes Health (clinical risk predictions), or Bike Sharing (time-series forecasting).
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      
                3. Google BigQuery
                Direct GCP Cloud Ingest
                
      Pull tables directly from Google Cloud BigQuery. Supports 350+ public BigQuery datasets and private enterprise tables, automatically unpacking nested Structs and Arrays into clean tabular data.
      
                
      
                  
      🔑 Required IAM Roles for Import:
      
                  
        
                    
      • Public Datasets: Zero credentials required (uses zero-cost TableData sampling).
        • 
                    
      • Private Datasets: Requires roles/bigquery.dataViewer (BigQuery Data Viewer) and roles/bigquery.jobUser (BigQuery Job User).
        • 
                  
      
                
      
              
      
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              ⚙️ Step 2: Configure & Choose Your Synthesizer
            
      
            Engine Selection
          
      
          
      
            SynthGauntlet offers two distinct synthesis engines. Here is how to decide which one to use:
          
      
          
      
            
      
              
      
                ⚗️ Copula / Marginal Synthesizer
                Recommended for ML
              
      
              
      
                Uses rank-preserving Gaussian Copulas, dependency-aware grouped sampling, and conditional marginal distributions. Dynamically recombines records to break one-to-one identity linkage while preserving multi-column relationships.
              
      
              
      
                👉 When to use: When you need maximum analytical utility for Machine Learning model training, business dashboards, or software QA where preserving real-world correlations is your top priority.
              
      
            
      

            
      
              
      
                🔒 Differential Privacy (DP) Engine
                Strict Compliance
              
      
              
      
                Splits a formal epsilon (ε) privacy budget across one-way marginals and two-way contingency tables, adding calibrated Laplace noise so the output mathematically bounds the influence of any single person.
              
      
              
      
                👉 When to use: When sharing data under strict regulatory mandates (HIPAA, GDPR) requiring a formal mathematical (ε) proof of privacy, accepting minor statistical noise in exchange for ironclad legal protection.
              
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      How to configure the remaining parameters:
      
            
        
              
      • Target Label: The primary prediction column (e.g. income, churn). The synthesizer ensures all relationships with this label are strictly preserved.
        • 
              
      • Quasi-Identifiers (QIs): Indirect identifying columns (e.g., age, zipcode, occupation) that an adversary could match against public voter or phone records.
        • 
              
      • Release Policy: Choose Balanced (standard default), Privacy First (tightest privacy ceilings), or Analytics First (highest utility floors).
        • 
              
      • Output Rows: Specify how many synthetic rows to generate (from 500 up to 10,000 rows).
        • 
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              ⚔️ Step 3: Multi-Agent Cast & Operational Flow
            
      
            Multi-Agent System
          
      
          
      
            When you click Forge Synthetic Data, four coordinated AI agents evaluate your data over multiple bounded rounds:
          
      
          
      
            
      
              1. ⚗️ Forge (Generator)
              
      Proposes 3 candidate synthetic datasets per round, exploring the best trade-offs along the Pareto frontier.
      
            
      
            
      
              2. 🕵️ Challenger (Privacy Attacker)
              
      Executes simulated adversarial attacks (nearest-neighbor linkage, membership inference) to uncover leaks.
      
            
      
            
      
              3. 📏 Auditor (Utility Evaluator)
              
      Calculates Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistical distributions, correlation deltas, and Machine Learning utility (TSTR).
      
            
      
            
      
              4. ⚖️ Arbiter (Judge & Orchestrator)
              
      Applies mathematical release gates, selects Pareto winners, and triggers Auto-Heal rescue if any gate fails.
      
            
      
          
      
          
          
      
            Live Round Telemetry Gauges (Active During Execution):
            
      
              
      
                📐 Utility Benchmarks (Want High):
                Real-time green gauges tracking TSTR utility ratio, Distribution fidelity, and Query equivalence as new candidate tables are scored.
              
      
              
      
                🛡️ Privacy Leakage (Want Low):
                Real-time attack gauges tracking Re-identification, Membership leakage, Attribute inference, Auxiliary linkage, and Near duplicates.
              
      
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              📋 Step 4: The Duel, Round by Round & Auto-Heal Rescue
            
      
            Execution Log
          
      
          
      
            During synthesis, the live section displays "The Duel, Round by Round" table alongside the Live Activity Stream:
          
      
          
      
            
      
              How to Read the Duel Rounds Table:
              
      
                
      
                  Round
                  Iteration number (up to 5 adversarial rounds).
                
      
                
      
                  Utility
                  Aggregated TSTR ML utility & SQL query equivalence score.
                
      
                
      
                  Fidelity
                  Marginal 1D distribution match (Target ≥ 82%).
                
      
                
      
                  Re-ID Risk
                  Nearest-neighbor breach rate (Target ≤ 1.0%).
                
      
                
      
                  Membership
                  Shadow MIA classifier AUC (Target ≤ 60%).
                
      
                
      
                  Gate
                  Green PASS badge if all criteria met, else Red FAIL.
                
      
              
      
            
      

            
      
              Auto-Heal Rescue Ladder:
              
      
                If the adversarial rounds finish without a fully certified candidate, SynthGauntlet doesn't abandon the run—it automatically activates the Auto-Heal Rescue Ladder:
              
      
              
        
                
      • Attempt 1 (Tighten): Increases recombination dials, adds slight numeric perturbation, and elevates rare category floors.
        • 
                
      • Attempt 2 (Generalize): Performs targeted bucketization of Quasi-Identifiers (e.g. 5-year age bins) and suppresses low-DCR rows closest to real records.
        • 
                
      • Attempt 3 (Second Generation): Re-synthesizes candidates across 4 distinct random seeds using the repaired blueprint to break local minima.
        • 
              
      
              
      
                🛡️ All rescued tables are re-audited and attacked on the original real split to prevent overfitting before certification is granted.
              
      
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              📈 Step 5: Privacy–Utility Frontier Chart & Trade-Off Slider
            
      
            Interactive Pareto
          
      
          
      
            The Privacy–Utility Frontier scatter plot visualizes every candidate synthesized throughout your run:
          
      
          
      
            
      
              Understanding the Chart:
              
        
                
      • Vertical Axis (Y): Utility score (higher is better — preserves predictive power).
        • 
                
      • Horizontal Axis (X): Privacy breach risk (lower is better — stronger anonymization).
        • 
                
      • Green Dots: Certified candidates that satisfied all utility floors and privacy ceilings.
        • 
                
      • Red Dots: Uncertified candidates that breached a ceiling or fell below a floor.
        • 
                
      • Purple Line: The Pareto Skyline connecting optimal candidates where utility cannot increase without adding privacy risk.
        • 
                
      • Shaded Region: Forbidden zone past the privacy ceiling or below the utility floor.
        • 
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      
                Using the Trade-Off Slider:
                
      
                  Located directly below the chart, the Trade-Off Slider allows data stewards to explore certified candidates in real time between:
                
      
                
      
                  🛡️ Max Privacy
                  ⚡ Max Utility
                
      
              
      
              
      
                ✨ Instant Switching: Dragging the slider dynamically updates the active release table for CSV download and BigQuery export without having to re-run synthesis!
              
      
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              ⚖️ Step 6: Dual Scorecard (Useful vs. Safe) & Fairness
            
      
            Unified Evaluation
          
      
          
      
            The Dual Scorecard is SynthGauntlet's core verdict panel, presenting side-by-side mathematical utility metrics and adversarial attack outcomes:
          
      
          
      
            
            
      
              📏 Useful Panel (Fidelity & Utility)
              
        
                
      • 
                  Distribution Fidelity (≥ 82%): Compares 1D marginal shapes using 2-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov distances for numbers and Total Variation for categories.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Utility Ratio / TSTR (≥ 85%): Train-on-Synthetic, Test-on-Real. Trains an ML model on synthetic data and tests on holdout real data to ensure predictive accuracy is preserved.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Query Equivalence (≥ 95%): Evaluates analytical SQL aggregate queries (SUM, AVG, COUNT, GROUP BY) across demographic subgroups.
                
        • 
              
      
            
      
            
            
      
              🛡️ Safe Panel (Privacy Attacks)
              
        
                
      • 
                  Re-Identification Risk (≤ 1.0%): Nearest-neighbor Distance to Closest Record (DCR) attack testing whether real records can be uniquely linked.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Membership Leakage (≤ 60.0%): Shadow binary classifier AUC testing if an attacker can detect whether someone was in the training dataset.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Outlier Linkage Exposure (≤ 10.0%): Attack on top 5% extreme multivariate outliers so rare individuals cannot be singled out.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Attribute Inference Advantage (≤ 25.0%): Probes whether an attacker knowing Quasi-Identifiers can predict sensitive target attributes.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Auxiliary Linkage (≤ 15.0%): Tests cross-table joinability against external auxiliary datasets.
                
        • 
                
      • 
                  Near-Duplicate Rows (≤ 10.0%): Identifies synthetic rows that too closely mirror real records (memorization protection).
                
        • 
              
      
            
      
          
      
          
          
      
            
      
              Fairness & Demographic Representation Breakdown:
              Advisory Review
            
      
            
      
              Directly below the Dual Scorecard, SynthGauntlet breaks down performance across demographic slices (e.g. age - 20, age - 30, age - 40):
            
      
            
      
              
      
                Demographic Representation (%):
                Compares raw training prevalence vs synthetic prevalence (e.g., Real 49.7% → Synthetic 33.7%) to ensure minority cohorts are not wiped out.
              
      
              
      
                Label Gap (%):
                Measures divergence in target label outcome rates per cohort. A WARN badge indicates a label shift ≥ 5%, while PASS indicates balanced parity.
              
      
            
      
          
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      
            
      
              📊 Step 7: Column Distribution Overlay (Fidelity Inspector)
            
      
            Visual Verification
          
      
          
      
            In addition to mathematical release gates, SynthGauntlet provides a Gretel-style Column Distribution Overlay directly inside the Dual Scorecard. This allows you to visually inspect how accurately each synthetic column preserves your original training data's empirical shape:
          
      
          
      
            
      
              
      
                
                Numeric Continuous Features
              
      
              
      
                Bins continuous data into 10 normalized histogram buckets across unified min/max bounds. Displays comparative relative frequencies (% of total rows) alongside Real vs. Synthetic Mean, Standard Deviation, and Spread.
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      
                
                Categorical & String Features
              
      
              
      
                Tracks the top categorical frequencies, distinct cardinality, and modal values, grouping tail categories into an "Other" bucket. Compares categorical representation proportions side-by-side.
              
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
            💡
            
      
              Use the Column dropdown in the overlay card to cycle through any column. A tight match between the Real (Blue) and Synthetic (Green) bars confirms that marginal probability distributions and outlier shapes were accurately preserved.
